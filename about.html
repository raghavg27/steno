About Steno

Updated 4 November 2026

Who makes it

I am Raghav Gupta. I built Steno on my own: the skill, the words on this site, the design and the code. There is no team and no investor behind it. When you send an email, I read it and I answer it.

Why it exists

Most people can tell when ChatGPT wrote something. The giveaway is not the grammar. It is the rhythm, the big opening lines, the lists of three and the other habits a model falls back on when it has nothing specific to say. I wanted my own AI to stop doing that, so I wrote the rules down. Steno is those rules, made to work in any AI tool.

What I promise, and what I do not

  • Steno keeps your facts and your numbers. It changes how the text sounds, not what it says.
  • I do not promise a score on any AI detector. Detectors change their models often. That is why the examples on the home page link to QuillBot and GPTZero, so you can check the results yourself before you pay.

Why the price is high

Steno is built for people who write for work. If you clean up AI drafts for clients every week, the time you save on a few drafts is worth more than the price. If you only use AI now and then, it is not worth it for you.
