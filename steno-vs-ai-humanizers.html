Steno vs AI humanizer tools

AI humanizers rewrite text after your AI writes it. Steno teaches your AI to write well from the start. Here is where each one is the better choice, with prices checked on 4 November 2026.

Updated 4 November 2026

The short answer

If you want to paste text into a web page and get a rewrite with no setup, a humanizer is easier. If you write with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini every day and want every reply to sound right from the start, Steno is cheaper over time and has no word limit.

Where the humanizers are better

  • No setup. You paste text into their site and get a rewrite. Steno needs an AI tool such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini to run in.
  • Detector promises. Undetectable AI lists "Passes AI detectors" on every plan and offers a refund if the text is flagged. StealthGPT says it beats every AI detector. Steno does not promise any detector score. If a detector score is the only thing you care about, those tools are built for it. Test their claims yourself.
  • A small free plan. WriteHuman gives a few free requests every month. Steno has no free plan. The site shows real examples instead.
  • Detection built in. WriteHuman includes AI detection checks. Steno links you to QuillBot and GPTZero instead.

Where Steno is better

  • One payment. The humanizers bill every month or every year. Steno costs ₹4,999 / $56 once, with no renewal.
  • No word limit. Steno runs inside the AI tool you already use, so it has no word or request limit of its own.
  • Clean text from the start. A humanizer fixes text after your AI writes it. Steno goes into your custom instructions, so your AI writes clean text in the first place. You can still run it on old text as an editing pass.
  • Your text stays in your tool. After you buy, your text goes only to the AI tool you already use, not to another company.

What to choose

  • You write with AI a few times a month: use a humanizer with a free plan.
  • You need a promise about one detector: try a humanizer that makes that promise, and test it.
  • You write with AI every day and want it to sound like a person every time: see real Steno examples.
