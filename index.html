Questions

Before you buy.

Will this beat AI detectors?

No promise is made about any detector, because detector scores move and none of them publish their method. What Steno does is remove the patterns that make a human reader suspicious. That is the part you can actually control.

Which tools does it work with?

Anything that accepts a system prompt or custom instructions. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor and most agent frameworks. The skills file follows the Claude Skills format and also works as a plain prompt.

Does it change what my text says?

No. The rule is that no claim gets dropped and nothing new gets invented. Text usually gets shorter because the ornament goes, not because information does.

Is my writing going to sound flat now?

Ornament and voice are different things. Steno removes the patterns a model reaches for when it has nothing to say. Your actual argument, jokes and opinions stay.
