Steno / Privacy

Privacy policy

Steno collects as little as it can. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no cookies.

Updated 4 November 2026

Who is responsible

Raghav Gupta, India. Email raghavg2705@gmail.com about anything on this page.

Text you paste in the demo

When you press Convert, your text goes to the Steno server and then to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude. Anthropic rewrites it and sends it back. Steno does not save your text. Anthropic handles it under its own privacy policy. Do not paste private or sensitive information into the demo.

Your IP address

To stop abuse, the demo server counts requests from each IP address for one hour. The count is kept only in the memory of the server and is gone when the server restarts. It is not written to a database. Vercel hosts the site and keeps short request logs under its own privacy policy.

Payments

Buy Me a Coffee handles every payment. Steno never sees your card or UPI details. Buy Me a Coffee shares with me the details it shares with every seller, such as your name, your email and the amount. I use them to send updates and to help you. Its privacy policy applies to the payment.

Email

If you email me, I keep the email so I can answer you and help you later.

Cookies and browser storage

Steno sets no cookies. The site saves two small values in the local storage of your browser:

  • steno-tries: how many free demo tries you have used.
  • steno-theme: the colour you chose for the 3D shapes at the top of the home page.

These values stay on your device. The server cannot read them. To remove them, clear the site data for this site in your browser.

Other services this site uses

  • Vercel hosts the site and runs the demo server.
  • unpkg.com sends the three.js library that draws the 3D shapes on the home page. Your browser connects to unpkg.com to get it.
  • Anthropic rewrites the text you paste in the demo.
  • Buy Me a Coffee, only when you choose to pay.
  • QuillBot and GPTZero, only when you press a check button. They are separate sites with their own policies.

No tracking

This site has no analytics, no ad pixels and no tracking scripts.

Your choices

Email me to see or delete the emails and payment details I keep about you. Buy Me a Coffee keeps its own copy of the payment record, and you can ask them about it too.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the changelog says what changed.
